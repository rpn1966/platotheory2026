### Access

You will be able to watch all talks live and submit questions via the chat function in the Microsoft Teams links provided. 

### Guidelines for online participants

Please keep your microphone muted unless invited to speak.
Use the chat box for questions during talks.
Ensure you are logged in with your full name so we can identify participants.

### Time Zone

The workshop runs on UK time (UT). Please adjust accordingly for your local time zone.

If you have any technical issues or accessibility requirements, please contact **R.P.Nelson@qmul.ac.uk**.
